Instead of rushing through the city as a passive passerby, you will embark on a dérive—a guided "drift" through the city where you let the architecture, ambient lighting, and surrounding streetscapes draw you in. As an observant observer in these spaces, your goal is to look past ordinary facades and feel how energy systems, airflows, and building design silently shape the way New Yorkers experience the built environment around them every day.   Working in small groups, you will explore a designated retrofit building to evaluate how well it meets the sustainability benchmarks of LEED v4. Using your smartphone as an auditing tool, you will track real-world environmental upgrades: green roofs that cool urban heat islands, low-flow water fixtures, LED lighting overhauls, and indoor air filtration systems. Treat every feature not as a dry technical specification, but as a real-world change that fundamentally alters the health and mood of the people who live and work inside.   This field exercise is about seeing your everyday city in a completely new light. By mapping out how these buildings manage water, electricity, waste, and sunlight, you uncover how smart green design can break wasteful habits and build healthier communities. Keep an eye on the details, respect the spaces and people you interact with, and bring back field notes that show how green building retrofits can transform both our city and our daily lives.
