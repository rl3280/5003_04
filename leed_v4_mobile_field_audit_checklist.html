LEED v4 Field Audit Checklist

Retrofit & Existing Building Audit

Field Trip Info

1. Location & Transportation (LT) 2 Credits
Alternative Commuting
Observe: Bike racks, electric vehicle charging stations, or nearby public transit stops.
Surrounding Density & Site Access
Observe: Walkability to nearby services (restaurants, services, residential density).
2. Sustainable Sites (SS) 3 Credits
Heat Island Reduction
Observe: High-reflectance roofing materials, green/vegetated roof sections, or shaded hardscapes.
Light Pollution Reduction
Observe: Shielded exterior light fixtures pointing downward to prevent light trespass.
Rainwater Management / Site Hardscaping
Observe: Permeable pavers, rain gardens, bioswales, or cisterns on-site.
3. Water Efficiency (WE) 2 Credits
Indoor Water Use (Fixtures)
Observe: Low-flow aerators on faucets, dual-flush toilets, or WaterSense labels.
Metering & Outdoor Water Use
Observe: Smart irrigation controls, drip systems, native landscaping, or sub-meters.
4. Energy & Atmosphere (EA) 2 Credits
Existing System Retrofits
Observe: High-efficiency HVAC units, retrofitted LED lighting, occupancy sensors, programmable thermostats.
Renewable Energy On-Site
Observe: Rooftop solar PV arrays, solar water heating, or ground-source heat pumps.
5. Materials & Resources (MR) 2 Credits
Waste Management & Recycling
Observe: Centralized multi-stream recycling stations (paper, glass, compost, e-waste).
Reused / Sustainable Materials
Observe: Salvaged interior elements, rapidly renewable flooring (bamboo/cork), low-impact finishes.
6. Indoor Environmental Quality (EQ) 2 Credits
Air Quality & Ventilation Controls
Observe: MERV air filters, CO₂ sensors, entryway walk-off mats, operable windows.
Daylighting & Views
Observe: Direct line of sight to outdoor views in occupied spaces, glare controls (blinds/shades).
