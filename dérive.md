We walk the same path away from where we sleep, and then back to it, almost every day.  Try doing something a little different this time.  Find a building along your way that you and your trades crew could retrofit to reduce its carbon footprint.
Try to find a building that you haven't paid attention to in the past.  Think about the building differently than you have in the past -- think about it in terms of material and energy waste that could be reduced if your crew built something in or around the building.  Imagine all the heat escaping leaking windows in the winter, and all the A/Cs on to offset the hot black tar roofs.

- Use the LEED checklist app to organize your observations of the building.
- Post your pics on Padlet
- Do the math!
